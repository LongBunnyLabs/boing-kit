Boing Behaviors & Bones

Single-point springs and bone-chain solvers with length, pose, and squash-stretch stiffness — plus optional twist propagation via swing-twist decomposition.

Effectors, Reactors & Fields

Point effectors push individual reactors or volumetric reactor fields that can be sampled on the CPU or via compute shader / shader subgraph on the GPU.

Three Render Pipelines

Ships as parallel Unity projects for the built-in, URP, and HDRP pipelines, kept in lockstep via robocopy-based sync scripts.
